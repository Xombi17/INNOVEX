Preserve the client-managed authorization transaction

Under RFC 7636, the client binds its secret code_verifier to code_challenge = BASE64URL(SHA256(code_verifier)) while state correlates the client transaction. Scopes are not part of that hash. Changing a pending request can desynchronize the client transaction; the client must initiate permission changes itself. ScopeBomb pauses before consent and provides least-privilege scopes for a fresh client initiation.
