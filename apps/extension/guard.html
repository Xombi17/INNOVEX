Why mid-flight scope rewriting breaks PKCE code_challenge

Under RFC 7636, the client binds its secret code_verifier to code_challenge = BASE64URL(SHA256(code_verifier)) and an immutable state token. Mutating scope= mid-flight in an extension without the client's verifier breaks the token exchange or causes silent mismatch. ScopeBomb pauses before consent and provides least-privilege scopes for a fresh client initiation.
