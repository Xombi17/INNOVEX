In the era of autonomous agents and shadow AI tools, credential management has hit a critical crisis: the Domino Cascade.

When developers grant OAuth permissions to AI agents, they frequently bridge multiple trust boundaries—connecting local configuration files (`.mcp.json`, `.env`), identity providers (Google, GitHub, Slack), and production cloud infrastructure. When a leak occurs, security teams rely on RFC 7009 revocation endpoints, assuming an HTTP 200 OK status means containment. In reality, secondary refresh tokens, app grants, and independent service sessions frequently remain active and uncontained.

ScopeBomb is the first Zero-Trust Blast-Radius Analyzer & Pre-Consent Guard designed specifically for the developer security lifecycle:

🔹 Real-Time Domino Attack Graph: Models multi-hop privilege escalation across 5 distinct trust boundaries with interactive real-time detonation.
🔹 Protocol-Accurate Containment: Distinguishes naive single revokes from comprehensive multi-issuer kill switches (including GitHub 204 grant deletion).
🔹 Proactive Pre-Consent Interception: MV3 extension sandbox blocks over-scoped permissions before consent is granted, preserving PKCE cryptographic hashes without corrupting state.
🔹 100% Client-Side Privacy: Zero backend databases, zero telemetry exfiltration, sub-100ms deterministic execution.
